# HIPE Manuscript Organization

The research manuscript is built from the registered evidence rather than serving as an independent store of manually updated results.

- `sources/manifest.json` identifies the supplied conference reference, the later server manuscript and the OpenReview URL.
- `journal/OUTLINE.md` connects the proposed journal argument to its required evidence.
- Conference source PDFs are preserved in ignored artifact storage and identified by SHA256. Original LaTeX source has not yet been registered.
- Final plots and tables should be generated by versioned analysis from registered runs, with a figure/table manifest recording the inputs.

The existing title is **The Hippocampal Place Field Gradient: A Bio-inspired Framework Building Multiscale Representation for Better Sample Efficiency**. A journal title and venue will be chosen after the extension contribution and evidence are clear. The conference PDF metadata does not establish journal or conference acceptance status.
